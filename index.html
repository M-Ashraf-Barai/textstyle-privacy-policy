TextStyle - Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 6, 2026

1. Introduction

TextStyle respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how the TextStyle mobile application handles user information.

2. Information Collection

TextStyle does not collect, store, or transmit users' personal information to any external server. The application does not require users to create an account or sign in.

3. Text Processing

TextStyle processes text entered by users locally on their devices to generate different text styles. The text entered into the application is not uploaded to an external server.

4. Clipboard Access

TextStyle provides a copy feature that allows users to copy generated text to their device's clipboard. This functionality is initiated by the user. TextStyle does not transmit clipboard contents to an external server.

5. Third-Party Services

TextStyle does not currently integrate advertising or analytics services.

6. Data Security

Since TextStyle does not collect or transmit personal information to an external server, it does not maintain a remote database of users' personal information.

7. Children's Privacy

TextStyle does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

8. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when application functionality or privacy practices change.
